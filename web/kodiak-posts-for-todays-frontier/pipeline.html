Creative Generation, Localization & Artificial Intelligence

Each campaign follows a dependable creative workflow: approved product images and brand guidance shape the concept, artificial-intelligence tools help develop imagery and language, localization adapts the message for each market, and finished files go to the approved asset library. live means available now; ready means prepared for a future release.

Design System Inventory Creative Generation, Localization & Artificial Intelligence Secure hosting and delivery

Creative generation live

The service selects the best available creative route for each request, returns approved campaign imagery, and keeps brand details under control. Each result includes a clear explanation of how it was made.

  • Approved product image live — uses an approved product image with the campaign headline, layout, and brand styling.
  • Nova Pro + Stable Diffusion live — Nova Pro creates the art direction and Stable Diffusion develops a photographic scene. Approved brand elements and final text receive controlled composition after image generation so the finished creative stays accurate.
  • Nova Pro + composed scene live — Nova Pro guides the concept while the system composes approved images and copy into a finished layout when a photographic route is unavailable or slower.
  • Brand-safe fallback live — a dependable on-brand layout keeps the campaign usable when a generation service cannot complete in time.

Artificial intelligence at work live

Each tool has a clear role. The workflow uses capable generation tools where they add creative value, then applies brand controls and approved content before delivery.

  • Grounded brand knowledge live — keeps creative direction connected to approved brand guidance, product language, and past approved examples.
  • Nova Pro art director live — turns the campaign idea into visual direction and headline options that fit the selected market.
  • Stable Diffusion image generation live — develops photographic variations from the art direction. Final text, product imagery, and approved brand elements are composed afterward for accuracy.
  • Nova Micro language support live — helps prepare localized market copy while leaving room for human review where a language or cultural context needs it.
  • Multimodal image matching live — helps find relevant approved product images and past campaign references. A secondary matching method supports continuity when the primary service is unavailable.

Localization live

Market and language guidance helps each campaign feel relevant to its audience. English source copy stays available when a localized service cannot answer.

  • Market guidance live — selected places contribute local context, seasonal cues, retailer choices, and language preferences.
  • Localized copy live — English, Spanish, Portuguese, and other market languages can appear with the campaign imagery. Person-reviewed language remains available where automatic translation is not appropriate.
  • Reusable corrections live — approved market-specific wording can be reused so future campaigns become more consistent over time.

Approved asset library + campaign files live

Approved product images, brand references, campaign files, and export sizes stay organized so teams can reuse trusted material.

  • Approved product images live — selected products can be placed into a scene with their approved appearance preserved.
  • Past campaign references live — teams can browse approved examples and use them as creative direction for a new idea.
  • Campaign outputs live — each campaign can produce the required social sizes, localized copy, filenames, and a downloadable preview package.
  • Review information live — each result keeps a plain-language record of its source image, creative direction, and localization choices.

Planned improvements ready

Prepared ideas that can extend the campaign experience as the product grows.

  • Expanded creative coordination ready — make more of the workflow available through one coordinated experience.
  • More transparent monitoring ready — give client teams clearer visibility into service health, generation choices, and review outcomes.

Brand updates guide

Client teams can keep the workflow current by maintaining approved products, recipes, images, market wording, and examples.

  • Add an approved product, recipe, or image — provide the product information, approved image, and usage guidance so the creative workflow can reuse it safely.
  • Review a market translation — approve or correct localized copy when local voice, cultural context, or legal review calls for a person to make the final decision.
  • Reuse an approved asset — select a trusted product image or past campaign reference to guide a new campaign while keeping the new brief distinct.
  • Promote a prepared improvement — review the change with the client team, confirm the expected behavior, then include it in the next repeatable release.

What happens when you create guide

One campaign idea becomes a reviewable set of creative options with market context, product choices, localization, and a clear record of the decisions behind each result.

  • Brief — describe the campaign idea, selected market, season, products, creative direction, and partner choices.
  • Creative direction — artificial-intelligence tools develop visual options while approved product images, brand elements, and final text remain under controlled composition.
  • Market adaptation — local context and language preferences shape the message. English source copy remains available when a localized service needs review.
  • Review and download — inspect the four export sizes, review how the result was made, then download the preview package for client feedback or the next production step.
