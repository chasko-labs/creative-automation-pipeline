# Eval: coach grounding graduation gate (Unit 5)

## Question

Does the prompt-grounded coach (knowledge.md in the Nova Micro system prompt)
answer well enough, or do we graduate to S3 Vectors RAG?

## Corpus

- `coach/knowledge.md` (regenerated by `scripts/build-coach-knowledge.py`):
  8 theme briefs + 4 retailer framings + section map + answer rules. ~2.8KB.
- Live traffic on `/insights` + `/ask` once the coach stack deploys.

## Method

After 2 weeks live (or 50 answered questions, whichever first):

1. Sample 20 `/ask` answers. Score each: correct (named the right chip/market/
   brief move), harmless-miss (right area, wrong slug), wrong (invented a
   theme/endpoint or misdirected).
2. Measure `knowledge.md` byte size and Nova Micro input tokens per call.
3. Check correction clustering: do misses cite content absent from knowledge.md?

## Graduate to S3 Vectors RAG iff

- Wrong rate > 15%, OR
- misses cluster on content missing from the prompt (proof the corpus, not the
  model, is the ceiling), OR
- knowledge.md passes ~8k tokens (prompt cost/latency outweighs retrieval).

Otherwise stay prompt-grounded. Plumbing (`KODIAK_VECTOR_*` on the generate
stack) already exists; graduation adds a query hop in `coach/index.mjs` only.

## Confounders

- Bedrock degradation days (throttled answers look like bad answers — exclude
  503 windows from the sample).
- Edits rejected at confirm time are UX signal, not answer-quality signal;
  count them separately.
