Propane griddles require propane to heat and
                         are a good choice for outdoor events or places where electricity
                         is difficult to access.

You cannot use propane griddles in indoor
                         spaces.

They are larger, heavy-duty, and make cooking in more
                         remote locations easier.

Pam or cooking oils must be used with
                         these griddles to avoid batter sticking and burning.

Knowing how
                         to safely use propane is necessary to use these griddles.
