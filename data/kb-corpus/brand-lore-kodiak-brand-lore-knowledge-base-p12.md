Heat your griddle to 375° F. You can test this by
            dripping a few drops of water onto your skillet’s
            surface; the drops should dance around a little bit
            before evaporating, signaling about 375° F.

While the griddle heats up, prep the batter. Start with
            about half a bag of mix and add 2 cups of water. Mix
            just enough to moisten the dry ingredients—lumps
            are okay and help hold the pancake together. The
            consistency of the batter should be thick but still
            thin enough to slowly pour into a batter bottle. Add
            water or mix as needed until the consistency is right.
            Always mix in a bowl then transfer to batter bottle.

Batter bottles are used to help manage mess and
            control the size of the pancakes. Make sure to only
            fill bottles ¾ of the way up. If you fill the bottle
            completely, your bottle will turn into a pancake
            batter volcano.
