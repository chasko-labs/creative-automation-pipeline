Kodiak Cakes’ cartons are manufactured with 20pt URB board which is made from 100% recycled paper fiber
and is recyclable. The higher density and stiffness of this board also results in excellent scoring and finishing
quality. URB board is easily recycled in most communities in the US and Canada. The recycling rate of paper
and paperboard in the US today is 66%* and consumers understand that paperboard is a highly sustainable
packaging material. Kodiak Cakes is committed to sourcing packaging from responsible partners using
renewable and recyclable materials. This aligns with their position as a rustic mountain-town natural food
brand. The choice of recyclable paperboard reflects the brand’s commitment to sustainability.

*Source: American Forest & Paper Association - 2020
