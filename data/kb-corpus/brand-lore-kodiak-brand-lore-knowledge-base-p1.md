BRAND AMBASSADOR PRINT GUIDE
Our mission to feed epic days and wilder
lives through a real breakfast starts with
one key ingredient — you guessed it — a
real breakfast. Founded in 1982 by Penny
Clark, Kodiak’s origin began when she
daringly sent her 8-year-old son, Joel
Clark, into the neighborhood with a red
wagon full of homemade flapjack mix in
paper bags. He returned with an empty
wagon and, well, the rest is history!

Our brand ambassadors help ignite our
breakfast fire by doing exactly that:
sharing a better-for-you, easier, and
tastier alternative to our community.
We’re stoked to go on this epic journey
with each and every one of ya.
           Who is Kodiak?
