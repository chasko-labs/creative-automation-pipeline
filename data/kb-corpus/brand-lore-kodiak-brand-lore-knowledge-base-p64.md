Premium Positioning: Transforming a Commodity Into a Growth Product For years, pancake mix sat in the middle of grocery store aisles as a low-priority, low-margin category.

Most products were built using refined flour, sugar-heavy formulas, and very little differentiation.

Kodiak Cakes disrupted this model by repositioning the entire category into a premium nutrition space.

Instead of asking “how cheap can we make it?”, the brand asked: “What if pancake mix could be healthy, functional, and performance-driven?” This mindset led Kodiak Cakes to build its product strategy around: 100% whole grain ingredients High protein content per serving No artificial fillers or unnecessary additives Functional nutrition for active lifestyles Because of this shift, Kodiak Cakes could justify a higher price point while still increasing demand.

In fact, many retailers found that Kodiak Cakes helped increase overall category revenue rather than just replacing existing products.

This is one of the biggest reasons Kodiak Cakes secured strong and stable retail shelf positioning.
