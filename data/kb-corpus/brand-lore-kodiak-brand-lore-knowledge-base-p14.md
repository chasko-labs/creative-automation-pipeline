        HIGHLIGHT OPERATIONAL DIFFERENCES BETWEEN THE ELECTRIC
        GRIDDLES AND THE PROPANE ONES:

We have two good options for cooking at an event—electric and
                         propane griddles.

Both are used to cook pancakes and heat frozen
                         waffles in the field.

Electric griddles require electricity to heat and
                         are a good choice at indoor events or events where electricity is
                         easily accessible.

They are smaller, nonstick, and do not need Pam
                         or cooking oils.

It is important to note that electric griddles can
                         only be plugged in 1 griddle to 1 outlet, or they will short circuit
                         and stop working.
