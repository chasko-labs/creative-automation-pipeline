            AFTER EVENT EXPECTATIONS
            After the event ends, griddles are unplugged, cooled down, then cleaned with paper
            towels and food safe disinfectant. Mixing bowls, spatulas, whisks, syrup bottles,
            and batter bottles are rinsed out and taken to a kitchen to thoroughly clean and dry.
            Anything sticky or dirty gets wiped down, and trash and recycling gets put in proper
            places. Tablecloths need to be washed. Items that don’t need to be cleaned are packed
            up and prepared as much as possible for the next event. Once everything is clean,
            all items must be brought back to the storage unit in Park City and put back in their
            place. Packing up the activation space is everyone’s job, and splitting up the work and
            cleaning duties helps make cleanup easier and faster. Teamwork makes the dream work.
