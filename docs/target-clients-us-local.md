# target clients — american founded/run, chasing local footprint

> flip from mx/ca -> us expansion to us -> local. these are us-founded insurgents that win by regional density, not national spray — and they need hundreds of localized social variants per month to do it.

## insight from research

- **bain 2025 insurgents:** 120 us insurgent brands captured 39% of incremental category growth on <2% market share (vs 17% in 2023). 43 newcomers this year; 103 additional emerging insurgents. scale insurgents now: Chobani, Rao's, Celsius (joining Monster, CeraVe, Blue Buffalo, e.l.f.). emerging: Goodles, Mike's Hot Honey, Cleveland Kitchen, Solely, Belgian Boys, cocojune. definition: >$25m revenue, >10x category growth, independent or recently acquired.
- **regional density playbook (cpg collective, matt williams):** focused geography improves marketing + distribution, buyer readiness, local sales data builds case for expansion — "model before taking on broader distribution" with geo-targeted marketing + ai data aggregation. this is how tacombi built cpg distribution around taqueria regions (ny, s-fl, mid-atlantic, chicago).
- **mid-market retail media:** quad reports walmart connect grew 29% but cpg marketers increasingly diversify to mid-market/local grocers — better local data, cheaper, more converting. this is the retail media footprint insurgents actually buy.
- **field marketing gap:** shelf love (nashville -> 80+ local teams nationwide, $10k -> $1m projected 2026 revenue) exists because emerging cpg brands get distribution then fail at local awareness — need local teams for demos/events + localized social to drive velocity per store.

## why "more local footprint" = hundreds/month for these

- retailer-specific packs for mid-market grocers (quad: diversifying from walmart connect) — each retailer wants its own social variant
- geo-targeted tiktok/meta by dma/zip (cpg collective: ai-driven data aggregation per region)
- flavor/occasion localization (chobani oat vs yogurt, mike's hot honey pizza vs chicken, goodles adult vs kids)
- field + social loop: shelf love demos in nashville need matching nashville social that same week — repeat per city
