# target clients — north america corridor: us, mexican, canadian

> addendum to target-clients.md — focus on mexican + canadian brands expanding into us local markets, plus us challenger cpg that still runs hundreds of localized social campaigns.

## why na corridor matters for this pipeline

- us is 330m + 62m hispanic consumers -> spanish/english localization is not "nice to have", it's ctr driver. wendy's x takis fuego meal, danone activia -> silk/danimals expansion to hispanic audiences shows always-on multi-brand campaigns.
- usmca + nearshoring makes mx/ca -> us retail velocity faster: 4,000-store somos rollout, siete in 40k retailers, deciem/the ordinary in 400 ulta + sephora.
- tier 2 mx/ca brands go from dtc/tiktok or regional grocery to needing sephora/ulta/target-grade compliance at 3 ratios — exactly the "no guide yet" window where our brand compliance module becomes the interim style guide.

## mexican brands expanding into us local markets

### tier a — proven us velocity, expanding still

1. **grupo bimbo / barcel usa — takis + marinela** — takis is the case study: bimbo's mexican hero driving us salty-snack share, now stretched to 5 extensions (waves, watz, pop!, stix, hot nuts). wendy's x takis fuego collab, hispanic heritage displays with takis. bimb bakeries usa president: bimbo+marinela = significant portion of sweet-goods business. hundreds of social variants is inherent to flavor/extension launches + hispanic heritage programming.
   - brand system: takis has strong pack system but each extension/collab tests new visual — fragmented.
2. **siete foods** — see above. pepsico completed acquisition jan 2025, now scaling via frito-lay/pep distribution. needs localized social for better-for-you + heritage + club vs grocery messaging.
3. **somos** — see above. 4k stores, amazon dtc, plant-based, family-farm sourcing story — needs region-specific social (tx/ca vs midwest).
4. **jarritos** — mexican soda (not in search snippet but archetype) — national us distribution, seasonal flavors, hispanic heritage tie-ins. similar to takis: flavor-led social cadence.

### tier b — newer mx -> us, thin brand book = sweet spot

5. **sarelly (sarelly)** — mexican beauty startup, $3m bridge round aug 2026, entertainment-led demand model (not paid media), hero franchise cow lashes launching to national us audience via retail expansion. quote: "most important retail expansion in our history" — ceo remi martini. zero成熟 us brand book, needs 3-ratio social packs per retailer.
6. **xebra brands / elements** — cpg exec hired to lead mx+us expansion for cannabis/wellness elements — example of wellness cpg using us+mx joint launch, needs compliant claims social (prohibited words gate matters).
7. **ricolino (ex-bimbo confectionery, now mondelez)** — bimbo sold ricolino to mondelez 2022 — shows mexican confectionery needing global social localization post-acquisition.

### why hundreds/month fits mx->us
- flavor/sku velocity (takis extensions), seasonal hispanic heritage moments, retailer-specific packs (target hispanic heritage endcap 250 stores featuring siete/honey baby etc), and spanish/english + regional us (tx/ca/fl/nyc hispanic density) localization.

## canadian brands expanding into us local markets

### beauty / cpg challengers

8. **deciem / the ordinary (toronto)** — estee lauder acquired fully june 2024 (tier scaling $500m-$1bn). niod, hylamide, loopha (30 products in development including pet/laundry). us path: deciem minority investment 2017 -> sephora -> 26 skus in 400 ulta stores july 2024 ("makes us more accessible"), pet/laundry expansion signals need for hundreds of social variants across sub-brands. loopha launches dtc only — thin style guide for new sub-brand at us scale.
9. **sapsucker (canadian maple/plant-based bev, not in search but archetype)** — dtc -> whole foods us; similar to somo: need localized us creative.
10. **tilray brands (nyse:tlry, toronto)** — from cannabis to diversified global cpg (beverage, cannabis, wellness). investor deck nov 2025: roadmap to diversified cpg powerhouse, q1 fy2026 return to profit. us play depends on rescheduling (280e, banking) — needs compliant social at scale, prohibited-terms gate critical.
11. **canada goose** — luxury outerwear doubling retail footprint (2023 target), planning to double stores + new categories. us is biggest market but soft traffic vs canada bright spot q3 fy2025 ($607.9m revenue). needs year-round social to stretch beyond parkas — again 3 ratios per category/region.

### broader canadian cpg corridor
- lululemon (athleisure) is not cpg food but cpg-adjacent lifestyle, shows canadian -> us retail velocity. less relevant for food/beauty pipeline but demonstrates corridor.
