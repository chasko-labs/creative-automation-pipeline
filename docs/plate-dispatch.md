# plate dispatch — concrete geometry per finished dish

Lesson (2026-09-15): naming the ingredient fails. SDXL cannot cash the
word "butternut squash" as a shape. What works is artist dispatch:
measured geometry (shape, size, arrangement) plus one plating frame.
Shape/size first, name last.

## dispatch frame (every plate prompt carries this)

- plating: round plate seen slightly from above, short stack of plain
  round flat cakes center plate, topping arranged over the stack, fork
  at the right side of the plate. No hand, no bite, no syrup pour.
- style tail: the frozen mono recipe (black ink only, white ground).

## palette law (binding on every dispatch line)

The model draws in black ink on white only. Any color/texture word in a
dispatch that lacks an ink rendering fights the mono negative and loses:
"browned edges" reads as *paint it brown*. Every sensory word must ship
with its ink translation, or be cut.

| never write | write instead |
| --- | --- |
| browned, caramelized, golden | dense crosshatching along the edge, faces left white |
| roasted, crisped | firm shapes with hatched undersides, clean unbroken outline |
| fresh, vibrant green | white leaves with center rib lines, wide spacing |
| juicy, glossy | one small white highlight gap per piece, no fill |
| red, orange, yellow (any hue) | cut. describe shape, never hue. |
| appetizing, delicious | cut. composition carries appetite, adjectives carry color. |

## geometry classes (shape/size/arrangement per class)

| class | geometry the prompt must state |
| --- | --- |
| cube/chunk | 1-inch firm cubes, dense crosshatched edges, white faces, piled 6-8 |
| floret | small tree-shaped florets, 2 inches tall, clustered 4-5 |
| spear/pod | long thin spears, fanned 3-4 in parallel |
| leaf | broad flat leaves, overlapping 2-3, visible center ribs |
| small round fruit | pea-sized round berries, scattered 5-7 |
| round fruit/veg | halves and wedges showing cut faces, 2-3 pieces |
| protein/mushroom | thick slices or whole caps, gill lines where cut |
| pourable | small pitcher alongside, still pool — never mid-pour |
| small loose | scattered handful, individual pieces distinct |
