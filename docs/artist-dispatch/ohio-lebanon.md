# artist dispatch — Lebanon, Ohio 45036 (US-OH-LEBANON)

Warren County orchard belt. Historic downtown and foraging woods, a few
degrees cooler inland. Retailer: Kroger. Audience: Warren County orchard
families, market regulars. Message: "Orchard belt frontier — protein for
harvest mornings."

## sourcing (family farms first)

- Lebanon Farmers Market — strawberries in May, sweet corn in July,
  peaches in August, apples in October, pumpkins in November, black
  walnuts in December; maple in February.
- Irons Fruit Farm (family orchard + cider); Hidden Valley Orchards
  (family orchard, August peaches, u-pick where posted).
- Farm stands and u-pick welcome wherever the farm posts them.
- Subscription: the Kodiak Cakes subscription service for rural
  communities — never a third-party CSA.

## place looks (year-round base)

Historic brick downtown under a small-town water tower; orchard rows in
tidy lines breaking to foraging woods; white farmhouses and red bank
barns; u-pick signs at the orchard gates. A few degrees cooler than the
river city; volatile spring; humid summer; crisp low-angle fall; gray
short winter days.

## animals (year-round base)

Cedar waxwings and robins working the orchard rows; eastern bluebirds on
the orchard posts; gray squirrels and cottontails at the wood edge;
white-tailed deer slipping the rows at dusk; barred owls in the foraging
woods; honeybees in the blossoms; fireflies over the orchard floor in
July.

## spring (Apr–May)

Place look: orchard rows white then pink with blossom; trillium and
bluebells in the foraging woods; volatile skies — sun, hail, sun again;
u-pick gates still chained; market tables full of starts and greens.
Animals: bluebirds claiming the posts; robins nesting in the rows; bees
loud; box turtles out; lambs (where the farm keeps them) in the side
pasture.
Looks: chore jackets, mud boots; paper flats; enamel mugs.
Touches: blossom sprig beside the plate; wooden flat staging; hand-lettered
u-pick sign (opening date) in the background cue.

### maple (Feb carry) — sugarbush oatmeal cups

- Dish: hot maple oatmeal in sugaring-season memory.
- Kodiak product: Protein-Packed Oatmeal (Maple & Brown Sugar).
- Method: stir hot; finish with a still pool from a small pitcher
  alongside (never mid-pour); sap-bucket staging beside the frame.
- Plate dispatch line: "round bowl center frame, still pool across the
  surface, small pitcher alongside, spoon at the right; steam as clean
  white gaps."

### strawberries (May) — u-pick strawberry shortcake stack

- Dish: May strawberry shortcake over flapjacks.
- Kodiak product: Buttermilk Power Cakes mix.
- Method: halve market strawberries; stack two cakes; spoon berries and
  their still pool over; u-pick flat staging beside the plate.
- Plate dispatch line: "two round cakes center plate, halved round fruit
  showing cut faces 5-6 pieces over the stack with one small white
  highlight gap per piece, fork at the right."

### spring greens — orchard-green savory cakes

- Dish: savory cakes with market greens and shaved asparagus.
- Kodiak product: Buttermilk Power Cakes mix, griddled savory.
- Method: griddle two thick cakes; overlap 2-3 broad flat leaves with
  center ribs; fan 3-4 long thin spears in parallel over.
- Plate dispatch line: "two thick round cakes center plate, broad flat
  leaves overlapping 2-3 with center rib lines, long thin spears fanned
  3-4 in parallel over, fork at the right."

## summer (Jun–Aug)

Place look: humid green orchard canopy; coneflower along the orchard
drives; u-pick gates open; peach ladders in the rows; corn walls on the
approach roads; haze hanging over the woods at dawn.
Animals: waxwings stripping early fruit; fireflies over the orchard floor;
swallows over the barn; bees on the clover alleys; fawns at the wood
edge.
Looks: short sleeves, sun hats; paper pecks and half-bushels; cold enamel
mugs.
Touches: peach fuzz rendered as clean outline with sparse stipple; ladder
against the row in the background cue; one whole peach beside the board.

### sweet corn (Jul) — orchard-road charred-corn cakes

- Dish: charred sweet-corn savory Power Cakes.
- Kodiak product: Buttermilk Power Cakes mix, griddled savory.
- Method: cut July corn off the cob; fold kernels into batter; griddle
  small thick cakes; scatter kernels 8-10 distinct over; fan 3 charred
  scallions in parallel across.
- Plate dispatch line: "three small thick round cakes center plate,
  small loose kernels scattered 8-10 distinct, 3 long thin spears fanned
  in parallel across, fork at the right."

### peaches (Aug) — Hidden Valley peach cobbler waffle bake

- Dish: August peach cobbler waffle bake (the orchard-belt signature).
- Kodiak product: Power Waffles (Buttermilk) torn as the cobbler base, or
  Buttermilk Power Cakes mix baked cobbler-style.
- Method: slice Hidden Valley August peaches into wedges showing cut
  faces; bake over the waffle base; serve one square showing the cut face;
  wedges 2-3 over.
- Plate dispatch line: "one square bake showing cut face center plate,
  wedges showing cut faces 2-3 over the face, fork at the right; cut
  faces white with one highlight gap each."

### blackberries — foraging-woods berry Bear Bites

- Dish: Bear Bites with foraged blackberries for cubs.
- Kodiak product: Bear Bites (graham).
- Method: arrange Bear Bites 5-6 distinct; scatter pea-sized round berries
  5-7; wood-edge staging (oak leaf beside the board, never on the food).
- Plate dispatch line: "small bear shapes 5-6 distinct one side,
  pea-sized round berries scattered 5-7 the other, no fork — finger food
  board."

## fall (Sep–Oct)

Place look: crisp low-angle light down the orchard rows; aster and
goldenrod on the wood margins; cider press running at Irons; apple bins
high; pumpkins at the gates; maple color in the foraging woods.
Animals: deer working the drops at dusk; turkeys through the woods;
squirrels cutting walnuts; waxwings on the last fruit; owls calling
earlier each evening.
Looks: flannel, beanies; wooden bushels; burlap; enamelware.
Touches: apple halves showing cut faces; cider jug staging; u-pick sign
flipped to closed in the background cue; crate stamps in overlay copy.

### apples + cider (Oct) — Irons cider-apple compote cakes

- Dish: cider-simmered apple compote over Power Cakes.
- Kodiak product: Buttermilk Power Cakes mix.
- Method: dice Irons apples to 1-inch firm cubes; simmer in cider down to
  a spoonable compote; pile 6-8 over a stack of three plain round flat
  cakes; cider jug alongside as staging.
- Plate dispatch line: "stack of three plain round flat cakes center
  plate, 1-inch firm cubes with dense crosshatched edges and white faces
  piled 6-8 over the stack, fork at the right."

### pumpkins — gate-pumpkin oat muffins

- Dish: pumpkin-oat muffins with pepita crunch.
- Kodiak product: Kodiak Pumpkin Spice Muffin mix.
- Method: bake; scatter small loose toasted seeds distinct over each;
  serve two with one halved showing stippled crumb; pumpkin gate staging
  beside the plate.
- Plate dispatch line: "two round muffins center plate, one halved
  showing cut face with stippled crumb, small loose seeds scattered
  distinct, fork at the right."

### winter squash — roasted squash + walnut waffles

- Dish: roasted squash cubes with black walnuts over Power Waffles.
- Kodiak product: Power Waffles (Buttermilk).
- Method: roast squash to 1-inch firm cubes; pile 6-8 over a toasted
  waffle; scatter halved nuts 3 distinct.
- Plate dispatch line: "one square waffle with grid lines center plate,
  1-inch firm cubes with dense crosshatched edges piled 6-8, halved round
  nuts 3 distinct over, fork at the right."

## winter (Nov–Feb)

Place look: gray short days; bare orchard rows in strict lines; red barns
against snow; wreaths on the downtown doors; orchard gates chained;
woodsmoke over the farmhouses.
Animals: cardinals and juncos at the feeders; deer yarding at the wood
edge; owls calling at dusk; rabbits printing the snow between rows.
Looks: wool, heavy mugs, steam; cast iron; kraft and wood staging.
Touches: walnut bowls; whole spices; snow on the fence line in the
background cue; steam as white gaps.

### pumpkins (Nov carry) — November pumpkin bake

- Dish: baked pumpkin oatmeal for short gray days.
- Kodiak product: Protein-Packed Oatmeal or Pumpkin Spice Muffin mix baked
  thick in a ramekin.
- Method: bake thick; top with small loose toasted seeds distinct; spoon
  at the right.
- Plate dispatch line: "round ramekin center frame, small loose seeds
  scattered distinct over the surface, spoon at the right."

### black walnuts (Dec) — black-walnut apple crisp cakes

- Dish: stored-apple crisp with black-walnut topping over cakes.
- Kodiak product: Buttermilk Power Cakes mix (base) + oat-walnut crumble.
- Method: bake diced stored apples under the crumble; spoon over two
  cakes; small loose crumble distinct over the top.
- Plate dispatch line: "two round cakes center plate, 1-inch firm cubes
  with dense crosshatched edges 5-6 plus small loose crumble distinct
  over the stack, fork at the right."

### maple + snow — February sugaring cakes

- Dish: maple-topped flapjacks in sugaring month.
- Kodiak product: Buttermilk Power Cakes mix.
- Method: stack three plain round flat cakes; spoon maple still pool from
  a small pitcher alongside (never mid-pour); snow-fence and sap-bucket
  staging beside the frame.
- Plate dispatch line: "stack of three plain round flat cakes center
  plate, still pool across the top cake, small pitcher alongside, fork at
  the right."

## key holidays

### Easter — blossom-row berry minis

- Dish: silver-dollar minis with strawberries; Bear Bites for cubs.
- Kodiak product: Buttermilk Power Cakes mix (minis) + Bear Bites.
- Method: griddle minis 5-6 overlapping; halved strawberries showing cut
  faces 5-6 over; blossom sprig beside the plate.
- Scene: orchard rows in blossom; bluebirds on the posts; volatile sky
  breaking to sun.
- Plate dispatch line: "small round cakes 5-6 overlapping center plate,
  halved round fruit 5-6 pieces over, fork at the right."

### Memorial Day — u-pick opening grill cakes

- Dish: grilled savory cakes for the u-pick opening weekend.
- Kodiak product: Buttermilk Power Cakes mix, griddled savory.
- Method: grill thick slices to firm shapes with hatched undersides; lay
  2-3 over two cakes; u-pick gate-open staging.
- Scene: gates open; first berries near; swallows over the barn.
- Plate dispatch line: "two round cakes center plate, thick grilled
  slices with hatched undersides 2-3 over the stack, fork at the right."

### July 4th — orchard cookout corn + peach double

- Dish: charred-corn savory cakes plus August-early peach stack.
- Kodiak product: Buttermilk Power Cakes mix.
- Method: savory corn cakes as in the summer entry; alongside, a two-cake
  stack with peach wedges showing cut faces 2-3. Geometry carries the
  holiday — no hue words.
- Scene: orchard cookout; fireflies over the orchard floor; corn walls on
  the approach roads.
- Plate dispatch line: "two round cakes center plate, wedges showing cut
  faces 2-3 over the stack, small loose kernels scattered distinct,
  fork at the right."

### Halloween — cider-press pumpkin muffins

- Dish: pumpkin-oat muffins with seed crunch, cider staging.
- Kodiak product: Kodiak Pumpkin Spice Muffin mix.
- Method: bake; small loose toasted seeds scattered distinct; cider jug
  and apple bins beside the plate.
- Scene: press running at Irons; low-angle light down the rows; owls at
  dusk.
- Plate dispatch line: "two round muffins center plate, small loose seeds
  scattered distinct over each, fork at the right."

### Thanksgiving — Hidden Valley peach-orchard apple pie bake

- Dish: apple-pie bake with oat-walnut streusel.
- Kodiak product: Buttermilk Power Cakes mix (bake base) + Kodiak Granola
  streusel + black walnuts.
- Method: bake orchard apples under the streusel; serve one wedge showing
  the cut face; small loose streusel distinct over the face.
- Scene: orchard-belt table; deer at the wood edge in the background cue;
  walnut bowls.
- Plate dispatch line: "one wedge showing cut face center plate, small
  loose streusel pieces scattered distinct over the face, fork at the
  right."

### Christmas — snow-row cinnamon cakes

- Dish: cinnamon-swirl Power Cakes with walnut sprinkle and maple
  still-pool.
- Kodiak product: Buttermilk Power Cakes mix with cinnamon swirl.
- Method: griddle three cakes with center swirl lines set; spoon maple
  still pool from a small pitcher alongside; chopped walnuts 5-6 distinct.
- Scene: bare rows in lines under snow; red barn; wreath on the downtown
  door; steam as white gaps.
- Plate dispatch line: "stack of three plain round flat cakes with
  center swirl lines, still pool across the top, small loose nut pieces
  5-6 distinct, small pitcher alongside, fork at the right."
