# artist dispatch — Park City, Utah 84098 (Wasatch Back)

Market: US-MW-PARKCITY-84098. Retailers: Target + Walmart (Kimball Junction,
UT-224), Smith's Food & Drug (Park City); halo: Lee's Market, Heber/Kamas
natural grocers. Costco is a Salt Lake halo, not in-town.
Audience: mountain families (weekend griddle, cubs, cast iron at the
cabin), Wasatch trail households (alpenglow starts, trail fuel), resort
staff (Park City Mountain Resort + Deer Valley, early shift).
Subscription: the Kodiak Cakes subscription service (Subscribe & Save, 15%
off + free shipping over $45) — pantry boxes delivered to cabins and rural
routes up the Heber/Kamas corridor where the nearest store is an hour out.
Never a third-party CSA; farms below are ingredient sources only.
Ingredient sources (family farms preferred): Jensen Farms + Smith Orchards
via Park City Farmers Market (base of Park City Mountain Resort, Wed 11–5
through mid-Oct); Copper Moose Farm Stand (84098, biodynamic, Tue/Thu 12–6
Sat 9–3 Jun–Oct); Tagges Fruit Stand (SR-224 near Canyons Village, daily
through Sep, preserves year-round); Ballerina Farm (Kamas, farm butter +
raw Utah honey, daily to 8 PM).
Fresh window: June–October. November–March is storage + value-added
(apples, roots, squash, honey, butter) — flagged honestly per USU Extension
high-mountain calendar, never drawn as fresh mid-winter harvest.

## global frame (every plate prompt carries this)

- plating: round plate seen slightly from above, short stack of plain round
  flat cakes center plate, topping arranged over the stack, fork at the
  right side of the plate. No hand, no bite, no syrup pour.
- setting default: cast iron or cabin table in the foreground, Wasatch
  alpenglow / pine / boulder negative space behind, sky negative space at
  top for text. Bear at respectful distance or tracks only — no costume
  grizzlies.
- style tail: black ink only on white ground (frozen mono recipe).
- palette law (`docs/plate-dispatch.md`): every sensory word ships with its
  ink translation. Shape/size first, ingredient name last. Never write hue
  words; write density, spacing, and line work.

## place constants (year-round Park City, ~7,000 ft)

- Wasatch Back: alpenglow dawns, lodgepole/Engelmann spruce slopes,
  boulder fields, aspen groves, sagebrush benches. Main Street: mining-era
  false fronts and clapboard under the ski runs; lift towers on the
  ridgeline in winter.
- Year-round birds: mountain chickadee, Steller's jay, Clark's nutcracker,
  black-billed magpie, common raven, red-tailed hawk. Year-round mammals:
  mule deer, moose in willow bottoms (respectful distance). Year-round
  plants: Engelmann spruce, subalpine fir, Douglas-fir, big sagebrush,
  Gambel oak.

## 1. deep winter / ski season (Jan–Feb) — storage apples, honey-butter

What the place looks like: snow-loaded spruce, lift lines running, breath
fog at the trailhead. Dawn alpenglow runs pink over the ridgeline — render
as wide-spaced horizontal line bands, never hue. Kitchens run on storage:
bins of apples, jars of honey, crocks of butter. Resort staff eat at 5:30
AM before the lifts.
Animals present: gray-crowned rosy-finches at the high lifts, pine
grosbeaks in the crabapples, Bohemian waxwings in irruptive flocks on
mountain ash berries, snowshoe hares white against the spruce, pine
martens' bounding tracks (tracks only). Clark's nutcrackers caching.
Seasonal looks and touches: beanie at the door, headlamp on the bench,
enamel mugs, oatmeal cup on the lift shack shelf, Bear Bites in the pack.
Skis crossed outside the cabin door.

### 1a. storage-apple cinnamon cakes — ski-season card (Jan–Feb)

Recipe: 1 cup Buttermilk Power Cakes mix + 2/3 cup milk + 1 egg + 1/2 tsp
cinnamon. Core and 1-inch dice 1 storage apple (Jensen Farms cellar stock
via market vendors, Tagges preserves as backup); griddle-crisp in butter
until edges firm. Pile over the stack; dust cinnamon as fine stipple.
Protein note: 14g holds through a double shift.
Dispatch line: 1-inch firm cubes, edges worked with dense crosshatching,
faces left white, piled 6-8 over the stack, fine stipple dots across the
top cubes, no fill.

### 1b. honey-butter pool cakes (December–March value-added build)

Recipe: plain stack (1 cup mix + 2/3 cup milk + 1 egg). Whisk 1/3 cup raw
Utah honey (Ballerina Farm, Kamas) into 3 tbsp browned farm butter
(Ballerina Farm) to a still pool; pour tableside, pitcher alongside. This
is the resort-break-room plate — fast, warming, one hand free.
Dispatch line: short stack center plate with a still white ring pool at the
base, pool surface carrying one white highlight gap; one small white square
melting still at the stack crown, soft outline, no drip.

### 1c. root-vegetable hash cakes (January savory)

Recipe: batter with 1 tbsp melted butter. Peel and 1-inch dice carrots and
parsnips (Copper Moose storage bins); griddle-crisp, fold half into batter,
pile rest over stack with cracked pepper and a fried egg.
Dispatch line: 1-inch firm cubes piled 6-8, dense crosshatched edges,
white faces; one round egg white with clean unbroken outline beside the
stack.

## 2. spring thaw (Mar–May) — rhubarb first, mud season

What the place looks like: snow retreating up the slopes in dirty bands,
Mid Mountain trail still closed, Main Street slush to mud. First green on
the benches: sagebrush buds, willow catkins in the bottoms. Copper Moose
opens; rhubarb is the first fresh thing.
Animals present: mountain bluebirds back on the fence posts (males drawn as
smooth dark-backed perching shapes, no hue words), violet-green swallows
over the ponds, broad-tailed hummingbirds at early feeders, mule deer does
dropping fawns in the oak brush (distance only), yellow-bellied marmots
emerging at the high rocks.
Seasonal looks and touches: mud boots at the door, trail-closure signs,
market tote waiting. Easter table with a folded cloth napkin; rhubarb pie
cooling for supper, compote for breakfast.
Plants: willow catkins, sagebrush bud-break, first glacier-lily leaves in
the melt bands, serviceberry white bloom late May.

### 2a. rhubarb-orange compote cakes — Easter card (May–June)

Recipe: 1 cup Power Cakes mix + 2/3 cup milk + 1 egg. Dice 2 cups rhubarb
(Copper Moose early stalks), simmer 12 minutes with orange juice and light
honey (imported citrus tradition, noted) to a tart compote. Spoon over the
stack — still pool, never mid-pour. Easter build adds a whipped-cream
dollop.
Dispatch line: long thin spears cut to short batons, fanned 3-4 in
parallel over the stack plus a still white ring pool at the base with
stippled edge; batons drawn with fine parallel fiber lines, faces white;
Easter build adds one round white dollop, smooth outline, center plate.

## 3. early summer (Jun) — raspberries, lupine, trailheads open

What the place looks like: trails open (Armstrong, Spiro, Mid Mountain),
wildflower slopes starting — lupine spikes and Indian paintbrush among the
aspen trunks. Market in full swing Wed 11–5. Long light to 9 PM; sunrise
starts before 6.
Animals present: western tanagers in the firs, hummingbirds constant,
marmots whistling on the boulder fields, elk cows with calves high,
moose in the willows at dawn.
Seasonal looks and touches: headlamp-off at dawn, trail bench stack photo,
Bear Bites in the pack, market crate of raspberries on the tailgate.
Enamel mugs, pine needles on the table.
Plants/flowers: lupine, Indian paintbrush, columbine in the damp draws,
arrowleaf balsamroot big blooms on the benches, chokecherry bloom.

### 3a. raspberry scatter cakes (June)

Recipe: 1 cup Power Cakes mix + 2/3 cup milk + 1 egg; fold 3/4 cup
raspberries (Jensen Farms via the Wednesday market) into the batter so some
break on the griddle. Top with fresh berries and a still pool of lightly
crushed berries at the base.
Dispatch line: pea-sized round berries, scattered 5-7 over the stack, each
a ring of small stippled drupelet dots around a white center with one
small highlight gap; broken berries as open stippled rings.

## 4. high summer + July 4th (Jul) — Utah peaches, sweet corn

What the place looks like: full Wasatch summer — fireweed along the runs,
monsoon clouds building by afternoon, alpenglow starts at 5:45. Peach
tables at the market (Utah fruit from the lower valleys via Smith
Orchards); sweet corn by mid-July. July 4th: Main Street parade, fireworks
over the resort, pancake breakfast on the lawn before the parade.
Animals present: Clark's nutcrackers in the whitebark, violet-green
swallows over the beaver ponds, pika haypiles growing in the high talus,
mule deer bucks in velvet at dusk, bats over Main Street at dark.
Seasonal looks and touches: July 4th build — small cloth bunting on the
cabin rail behind the plate, never on it. Corn husks in a tub, peach flats
on the counter, subscription box open on the porch with the Family Size bag
— corridor delivery cue.

### 4a. peach preserve spoon cakes — July 4th card (July–September)

Recipe: 1 cup Power Cakes mix + 2/3 cup milk + 1 egg + pinch cinnamon. Fold
diced peach (Smith Orchards via the market, Jensen Farms); griddle. Top
with a spoon of warm Tagges peach preserve and a cinnamon stipple. Cast
iron serving cue.
Dispatch line: halves and wedges showing cut faces, 2-3 wedges fanned over
the stack, cut faces white with pit-cavity curve line, edges dense
crosshatch, one highlight gap per wedge; preserve as a still white ring
pool with stippled edge at the base.

### 4b. sweet corn griddle cakes (July–August savory)

Recipe: batter with melted butter; fold 3/4 cup cut sweet corn (market
tables) + 2 tbsp diced peppers removed before serving — corn only. Griddle
until edges firm; top with more corn and a knob of Ballerina Farm butter
melting still.
Dispatch line: small loose kernels, scattered handful of 10-12, individual
pieces distinct, each a small rounded square with a single germ-dot line,
piled over the stack; one small white square melting still at center.

## 5. late summer (Aug) — summer squash, asters, elk bugling early

What the place looks like: light going gold earlier, asters and rabbitbrush
starting on the benches, first aspen leaves turning at the very top.
Summer squash heaped at Copper Moose Saturday 9–3. First elk bugles echo
late August — rut card coming.
Animals present: ravens riding the evening thermals, broad-tailed
hummingbirds tanking up, marmots fat at the rocks, elk bulls gathering
harems high (distance or tracks for now — full rut card in fall).
Seasonal looks and touches: squash blossoms in a jar (blossoms beside the
plate, never on it), back-to-school lunchbox open with Bear Bites, market
crate stenciled with the farm name.

### 5a. summer squash ribbon cakes (August)

Recipe: batter as 1b. Shave 1 summer squash (Copper Moose) into long
ribbons; wilt 1 minute in butter with salt. Fold half into the batter, fan
the rest over the stack with cracked pepper.
Dispatch line: long thin ribbons, fanned 3-4 in parallel over the stack,
ribbons drawn with fine parallel line shading along the lower edge and
seed-dot stipple at the centers, faces white.

## 6. fall harvest + Halloween (Sep–Oct) — aspen gold, elk rut, apples

What the place looks like: quaking aspen gold on the slopes (render as
dense canopies with fine leaf-stipple, trunks as smooth white columns with
dark eye-dash marks), Gambel oak red in the understory, rabbitbrush yellow
bloom on the benches. Elk rut peaks late September — bugles at dawn. Market
runs to mid-October; apple bins, winter squash, last preserves. Halloween:
pumpkins on Main Street porches, first snow dusting the ridgeline.
Animals present: elk herds rutting in the high meadows (the live elk-rut
tile — respectful distance or tracks only on food plates), mule deer bucks
rubbing, black bears in the chokecherries before denning (distance only),
Steller's jays caching, flocks of mountain chickadees.
Seasonal looks and touches: Halloween build — one small whole pumpkin
beside the plate (never carved on the plate). Flannel back on, beanie at
the door again, market crate photo, apple press at the farm stand.

### 6a. winter squash cube cakes — Halloween card (September–November)

Recipe: 1 cup Power Cakes mix + 2/3 cup milk + 1 egg + 1/2 tsp pie spice
(imported tradition, noted). Peel and 1-inch dice winter squash (Copper
Moose field bins); roast to firm edges; pile 6-8 over the stack with
toasted seeds scattered.
Dispatch line: 1-inch firm cubes, edges worked with dense crosshatching,
faces left white, piled 6-8 over the stack; small flat seed ovals scattered
5-7, each with a center crease, solid light stipple.

### 6b. orchard apple wedge cakes (October)

Recipe: 1 cup Power Cakes mix + 2/3 cup milk + 1 egg + 1/2 tsp cinnamon.
Slice fresh apples (Smith Orchards, market through mid-Oct) into wedges;
griddle 1 minute per side in butter. Fan over the stack; finish with honey
still-pool in the side pitcher.
Dispatch line: halves and wedges showing cut faces, 2-3 wedges fanned,
cut faces white with seed-chamber star lines, edges dense crosshatch, one
highlight gap per wedge; small pitcher alongside, still pool inside.

## 7. Thanksgiving (Nov) — storage squash, apples, the cabin table

What the place looks like: snow line down to town, aspen bare white trunks,
spruce the only green. The cabin table: the good cloth, storage squash in
a crate, apple bins, honey jars. Thanksgiving morning stack before the
turkey goes in.
Animals present: moose yarded in the willows, pine grosbeaks in the
crabapples, gray-crowned rosy-finches at feeders, snowshoe hares fully
white, red foxes mousing the meadows at dawn.
Seasonal looks and touches: pie-safe open, cloth napkin beside the plate,
whole storage squash as table dressing (beside the plate), subscription
gift-box cue for the corridor cabins.

### 7a. storage-squash pie-spice cakes — Thanksgiving card (November)

Recipe: 1 cup Power Cakes mix + 2/3 cup milk + 1 egg + 1/2 cup mashed
storage squash (Copper Moose bins) + 1 tsp pie spice. Stack four high;
top with toasted seeds and a still pool of maple in the side pitcher.
Morning-after build wears leftover pie nuts.
Dispatch line: short stack built visibly taller (four cakes), faces white
with stippled crumb texture; 1-inch firm cubes with dense crosshatched
edges piled 5-6 over the top, small flat seed ovals scattered 4-5.

## 8. winter holidays + Christmas (Dec) — honey, butter, lights on Main

What the place looks like: Main Street strung with lights under the ski
runs, snow piled on the false fronts, lift towers blinking. Fresh produce
gone; the creative anchors are Ballerina Farm butter, raw honey, stored
apples, and warming spice. Christmas morning stack before the mountain.
Animals present: Steller's jays in the pines, mountain chickadees at the
suet, snowshoe hares, porcupine tracks in the snow under the spruce
(tracks only), moose at the willow bottoms at dusk.
Seasonal looks and touches: Christmas build — sprig of spruce with a cloth
bow behind the plate (never on it), lights bokeh behind, enamel mugs,
flannel. Resort-staff Christmas: oatmeal cup with a bow for the 5:30 AM
shift. Subscription gift-box cue: the Kodiak Cakes box with tissue,
corridor-porch delivery in the snow.
Plants: Engelmann spruce boughs, juniper sprigs, red-osier willow stems at
the creek (rendered as smooth lined stems, no hue words).

### 8a. Christmas morning honey-butter cakes (December)

Recipe: plain stack (1 cup mix + 2/3 cup milk + 1 egg + 1 tbsp melted
Ballerina Farm butter). Warm raw Utah honey with butter to a still pool;
pour tableside, pitcher alongside. Top with storage-apple fine dice and a
cinnamon stipple. Spruce sprig behind the plate only.
Dispatch line: 1-inch fine dice scattered 5-7 over the stack, dense
crosshatched edges, white faces, fine stipple across; still white ring pool
at the base with one highlight gap; small pitcher alongside.

### 8b. gingerbread-spice oatmeal cup — staff Christmas (December)

Recipe (oatmeal cup build, not a stack): 1 Protein Oatmeal Cup + hot water
to the line; stir in 1 tbsp Ballerina Farm butter, 1 tsp molasses, 1/2 tsp
gingerbread spice (imported tradition, noted); top with storage-apple fine
dice. Drawn in the cup, steam as three short vertical line curls.
Dispatch line: round cup seen slightly from above, surface white with
stippled spice dots, fine apple dice scattered 5 over the surface, one
small white square melting still at center; steam as line curls only.
