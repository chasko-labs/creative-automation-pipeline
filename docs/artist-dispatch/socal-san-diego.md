# artist dispatch — San Diego, California 92101 (US-W-SD)

Metro market with a mountain sister: frontier counterpart is Julian, CA
(Cuyamaca apple country) — mountain harvest flows downhill to city
tables. Retailers: Target, Vons, Whole Foods. Audience: surf families,
canyon trail runners — Amber. Message: "Surf frontier — protein for
wave mornings."

Markets: Little Italy Mercato (Saturday + Wednesday, the major weekend
market) is the city anchor; Julian farm stands and the Julian Certified
Farmers Market feed the frontier-sister line. Family farms first: Julian
Farm and Orchard, Volcan Valley Apple Farm, San Diego North County
avocado and citrus growers. City tables with no farm-stand week are
served by the Kodiak Cakes subscription service — never a third-party
box.

Monthly ingredient spine (repo data, retailer-frontier-pairs): stored
apples Jan; lowland citrus Feb; leafy greens Mar; asparagus Apr;
strawberries May; cherries Jun; peaches Jul; pears Aug; apples Sep;
Julian pumpkins Oct; late pears Nov; apple cider Dec.

How to read this file: every recipe ends with a DISPATCH line built on
the plate frame in `docs/plate-dispatch.md` (round plate slightly from
above, short stack of plain round flat cakes center plate, topping
arranged over the stack, fork at right, no hand, no bite, no pour) plus
the palette law (black ink on white only — shapes and counts, never
hue).

Year bird/plant/flower spine: Black-crowned Night Heron / Toyon /
Encelia in winter through Great Egret / Western Sycamore / Coastal
Goldenbush in fall. Balboa Park's Spanish-Colonial arches, Saltillo
tile, and bougainvillea are the built anchor; the Pacific and the
inland canyons are the wild anchors. Border food culture (Baja citrus,
lime, cotija) seasons everything.

## deep winter (Jan-Feb) + Mardi Gras

What the place looks like: crisp desert-clear mornings after Santa Ana
events, marine layer the rest of the time. Balboa Park's stucco arches
and tower hold long shadows; Saltillo tile darkens with rain. Backyard
citrus glows in every canyon-rim neighborhood; toyon berries full red
in the canyon scrub; Encelia rosettes silvering the coastal bluffs.
Mission Bay flat and gray-green; surf slow and lumpy.

Animals present: night herons along the San Diego River mouth and
Mission Bay marinas; white pelicans on the bay; gray whales
southbound past Point Loma; Anna's hummingbirds year-round at
bougainvillea; sea lions hauled at La Jolla Cove.

Seasonal looks and touches: wetsuit tops draped over balcony rails;
gas-fire-pit evenings; Mardi Gras (Feb) in the Gaslamp — keep to a
bead-and-trumpet table touch, never a parade scene.

### recipe: stored-apple + cinnamon keeper cakes (Jan)

Base: Kodiak Cinnamon Oat Power Cakes. Flesh: stored Julian apples
(Jan carry-over, firm keepers). Method: cut 1 apple into 1-inch firm
cubes; saute in butter with cinnamon bark 4 minutes till edges firm;
stack three cakes, pile cubes 6-8 over the top, still-pool cider syrup
in a small pitcher alongside.

DISPATCH: "stack of three plain round flat cakes center round plate;
1-inch firm cubes with dense crosshatched edges and white faces, piled
6-8 over the stack; small pitcher alongside with still pool; fork at
the right."

### recipe: lowland citrus + ricotta bright cakes (Feb, Mardi Gras)

Base: Kodiak Buttermilk Power Cakes. Flesh: lowland San Diego County
citrus — navel orange, Meyer lemon (Feb peak). Method: whip ricotta
with lemon zest threads; stack three cakes with ricotta between
layers; shingle supreme orange wheels 3 overlapping on top; zest
thread scatter 8-10 distinct. Mardi Gras touch: three small
bead-rounds at the plate rim only — never on the food.

DISPATCH: "stack of three plain round flat cakes center round plate;
round citrus wheels showing open cut faces, 3 pieces shingled
overlapping the top; thin curled threads scattered 8-10 distinct;
fork at the right."

## maple / spring (Feb-Mar) + Easter (Apr-May)

What the place looks like: the city's greenest weeks — winter rain
wakes the canyons, Encelia daisies and lupine on the bluffs, wild
cucumber draping the coastal sage. Balboa Park gardens at peak bloom;
jacaranda still weeks out. Easter brunch spills onto Saltillo-tile
patios under bougainvillea.

Animals present: herons and egrets nesting at the river mouth rookery;
osprey fledging over Mission Bay; northbound gray-whale calves close
offshore (Mar-Apr); swallow colonies back under every eave; fence
lizards on the tile.

Seasonal looks and touches: market totes heavy with greens; patio
umbrellas up for the first time; kite specks over Mission Bay.

### recipe: canyon greens + avocado savory cakes (Mar)

Base: Kodiak Buttermilk Power Cakes, griddled thin. Flesh: leafy
greens (Mar) + early Hass avocado — San Diego County is a top US
avocado county. Method: slice half an avocado into 4 smooth fans; wilt
greens with green onion; stack two cakes, lay greens flat, fan avocado
over the top, lime wedge at plate edge, chili-flake dots 5-7.

DISPATCH: "two plain round flat cakes center round plate; broad flat
wilted leaves with center ribs spread over the stack; smooth crescent
slices fanned 4 in parallel overlap on top; one wedge with cut face at
the plate edge; fork at the right."

### recipe: asparagus + lemon Baja cakes (Apr)

Base: Kodiak Buttermilk Power Cakes, griddled thin. Flesh: asparagus
(Apr lowland). Method: blister spears on the griddle; stack two cakes,
fan 3-4 spears parallel across the top, lemon zest threads, cotija
lumps 5-7 pea-sized, olive-oil still pool.

DISPATCH: "two plain round flat cakes center round plate; long thin
spears fanned 3-4 in parallel across the stack; 5-7 pea-sized round
lumps scattered among the spears; thin curled threads 6-8 distinct on
top; fork at the right."

### recipe: Easter strawberry + lime cakes (May)

Base: Kodiak Strawberry or Buttermilk Power Cakes. Flesh: coastal
strawberries (May). Method: quarter 8-10 berries; macerate with lime
juice + pinch sugar; stack three cakes, pile berries 6-8, lime zest
threads, cream quenelle. Easter touch: serve patio-side under
bougainvillea — blossoms stay out of frame.

DISPATCH: "stack of three plain round flat cakes center round plate;
quartered berry wedges showing open cut faces, piled 6-8 over the top;
one smooth cream oval beside the pile; thin curled threads 6-8
distinct on top; fork at the right."

## early summer (Jun)

What the place looks like: May gray into June gloom — the marine layer
sits till noon, Balboa Park's tower fading in and out, surfboards
appearing out of the mist at Tourmaline. Then the burn-off and hard
blue. Canyon sage smells sharp; first bougainvillea avalanche over
stucco walls.

Animals present: least terns over the bay mouth; brown pelican
squadrons; dolphins in the surf zone most dawns; leopard sharks
gathering off La Jolla (real summer aggregation); baby ground
squirrels at the park cliffs.

Seasonal looks and touches: hoodies knotted at waists; dawn-patrol wax
combs; market cherry pints stacked high.

### recipe: cherry + almond mountain cakes (Jun)

Base: Kodiak Almond Poppyseed or Buttermilk Power Cakes. Flesh:
mountain cherries (Jun, small-volume Julian-highland orchards).
Method: halve and pit 10-12 cherries showing open pitside faces; stack
three cakes, pile cherry halves 6-8, slivered-almond scatter 8-10 thin
flats, still-pool cherry juice in a small pitcher alongside.

DISPATCH: "stack of three plain round flat cakes center round plate;
small round halves showing open center pits, piled 6-8 over the stack;
8-10 thin flat slivers scattered distinct among the halves; small
pitcher alongside with still pool; fork at the right."

## high summer (Jul) + July 4th

What the place looks like: full surf city — white sand, blue water,
boardwalk bikes, Balboa Park fountains running, Cabrillo Bridge stark
against noon sky. Warm nights; Santa Ana teasers at sunset. Chalk
dudleya powdery on the Point Loma cliffs.

Animals present: grunion runs on moon tides (crowds with buckets,
hands-only); sea lions loud at the Cove; pelicans dive-bombing bait;
night herons retreating to day roosts.

Seasonal looks and touches: July 4th over Mission Bay and Glorietta
Bay — daytime parade, dusk blanket grid. Peach crates perfuming the
Mercato.

### recipe: peach + Baja-lime dawn-patrol cakes (Jul)

Base: Kodiak Buttermilk Power Cakes. Flesh: peaches (Jul). Method:
cut 1 peach into 6 wedges showing cut faces; griddle wedges 60 seconds
a side for firm hatched undersides; stack three cakes, shingle wedges
overlapping, lime wedge at plate edge, mint-leaf pair.

DISPATCH: "stack of three plain round flat cakes center round plate;
long wedge shapes showing open cut faces, 4-5 shingled overlapping
across the top with hatched undersides; one wedge with cut face at the
plate edge; one pair of small flat leaves with center ribs at the
crown; fork at the right."

## late summer (Aug)

What the place looks like: hottest water, thinnest crowds on weekdays,
gold light by six. Hills cured straw; sycamores along the river holding
green with peeling trunks; first goldenbush buds on the bluffs. Evening
glass-off on the bay.

Animals present: egret counts climbing at the river mouth; mullet
dimpling the channel; osprey teaching juveniles; tarantula males
crossing inland trails (note for trail scenes, never the plate).

Seasonal looks and touches: outdoor movies in the park; tomato seconds
cheap at the Mercato; wax melting in parked cars at the beach lots.

### recipe: pear + honey late-summer cakes (Aug)

Base: Kodiak Honey Oat Power Cakes. Flesh: pears (Aug). Method: slice
1 pear into thin half-moons; stack three cakes, fan 4-5 half-moons
overlapping, honey still pool in a small pitcher alongside, crushed
walnut scatter 5-7 distinct chunks.

DISPATCH: "stack of three plain round flat cakes center round plate;
thin half-moon slices showing seed-line centers, fanned 4-5 in
parallel overlap across the top; 5-7 small rough chunks scattered
distinct; small pitcher alongside with still pool; fork at the right."

## fall harvest (Sep-Oct) + Halloween

What the place looks like: Santa Ana crystal — visibility to the
Coronados and San Clemente Island, hot dry middays, cool nights.
Goldenbush in full gold along bluffs and canyon rims; sycamore leaves
parchment and dropping; bougainvillea second flush. Julian apples roll
downhill to city stalls. Halloween: Gaslamp + North Park porches
carved, haunted corn mazes inland.

Animals present: great egrets in numbers at Mission Bay; ibis on the
mudflats; Cooper's hawks over the park; coyotes at canyon rims at
dusk; southbound monarch clusters in the park pines.

Seasonal looks and touches: flannel over tees; apple-crate stacks at
the Mercato; paper-bat shop windows (props stay off the plate).

### recipe: Julian apple harvest cakes (Sep)

Base: Kodiak Cinnamon Oat Power Cakes. Flesh: fresh Julian apples,
September peak, from Julian Farm and Orchard / Volcan Valley Apple
Farm via city market stalls. Method: thin-slice 1 apple into wheels
showing star cores; stack three cakes, shingle 3-4 wheels overlapping,
cinnamon-dust lines (thin parallel strokes, not fill), cider still
pool in a small pitcher alongside.

DISPATCH: "stack of three plain round flat cakes center round plate;
thin round wheels showing star-pattern centers, 3-4 shingled
overlapping across the top; thin parallel dust lines across the wheels;
small pitcher alongside with still pool; fork at the right."

### recipe: Julian pumpkin + pepita cakes (Oct, Halloween)

Base: Kodiak Pumpkin Flax Power Cakes. Flesh: Julian pumpkins (Oct).
Method: roast 1-inch firm cubes; stack three cakes, pile cubes 6-8,
pepita scatter 5-7 distinct flat ovals, still-pool spiced syrup in a
small pitcher alongside. Halloween touch: carved pumpkin stays on the
porch — never in the frame.

DISPATCH: "stack of three plain round flat cakes center round plate;
1-inch firm cubes with dense crosshatched edges and white faces, piled
6-8 over the stack; 5-7 small flat seed ovals scattered distinct;
small pitcher alongside with still pool; fork at the right."

## Thanksgiving (Nov)

What the place looks like: low gold light, clearest air of the year,
bay glass green-gold. Night herons own the dusk at the marinas.
Sycamores bare and sculptural along the river. Pie steam on cold
windows in South Park; crab boats working the harbor.

Animals present: full winter bay roster — night herons, great and
snowy egrets, white pelicans, buffleheads and wigeons; late gray
whales far offshore; gull flocks on the bait dock.

Seasonal looks and touches: Friendsgiving patio spreads; apple-pie
boxes stacked at Julian-stand pop-ups; flannel blankets on the bay
grass.

### recipe: late-pear + apple-pie-spice cakes (Nov, Thanksgiving)

Base: Kodiak Cinnamon Oat Power Cakes. Flesh: late pears (Nov) with
Julian apple-pie tradition as the spice line. Method: poach pear
half-moons in spiced cider till firm; stack three cakes, fan 4-5
half-moons, crumble oat-crisp chunks 5-7 rough over the top, cider
still pool in a small pitcher alongside.

DISPATCH: "stack of three plain round flat cakes center round plate;
thin half-moon slices showing seed-line centers, fanned 4-5 in
parallel overlap; 5-7 small rough crumble chunks scattered distinct on
top; small pitcher alongside with still pool; fork at the right."

## winter holidays (Dec) + Christmas

What the place looks like: Balboa Park December Nights — the arches
strung with lights, food stalls elbow to elbow, the tower lit. Harbor
boat parade doubled in the water. Storm surf weeks brown the river
mouth; toyon at full red; Encelia silvered with rain; earliest
sunsets over the point.

Animals present: night herons hunched at every marina dusk; white
pelicans rafted tight; sea lions hauled on the bait barge; Anna's
hummingbirds on the last bougainvillea.

Seasonal looks and touches: luminarias on canyon-rim walls; tamale
steam at the Mercato holiday market; cider steam at every brunch.

### recipe: apple-cider + gingerbread-spice holiday cakes (Dec, Christmas)

Base: Kodiak Gingerbread or Cinnamon Power Cakes. Flesh: Julian apple
cider (Dec) reduced to syrup. Method: reduce 1 cup cider to a still
pool; stack three cakes, drizzle pool over the top (set, not pouring),
cinnamon-stick pair laid parallel beside the stack, star-anise single
at the crown, lowland orange wheel at the plate edge. Christmas touch:
park lights behind the diner — never in the frame.

DISPATCH: "stack of three plain round flat cakes center round plate
with a settled pool spread over the crown; two straight bark-length
sticks laid parallel beside the stack; one star-shaped pod with eight
distinct points at the crown; one round citrus wheel showing cut face
at the plate edge; fork at the right."
