# artist dispatch — Dayton, Ohio 45402 (US-OH-DAYTON)

Miami Valley maple corridor. Humid-continental, brick downtown and market
sheds. Retailer: Kroger, Target. Audience: Miami Valley families, 2nd
Street Market Saturdays. Message: "Maple corridor frontier — protein for
market mornings."

## sourcing (family farms first)

- 2nd Street Market (Saturdays) — maple in February, strawberries in May,
  sweet corn in July, tomatoes in August, apples in October.
- Downing Fruit Farm (family orchard); Dohner Maple Products (family
  sugarbush); Fulton Farms (family produce + u-pick); Patchwork Gardens;
  Mile Creek Farm (family farm stand).
- Farm stands and u-pick welcome wherever the farm posts them.
- Subscription: the Kodiak Cakes subscription service for rural
  communities — never a third-party CSA.

## place looks (year-round base)

Brick downtown blocks and open market sheds; the Great Miami River and its
wooded banks; flat valley fields breaking to low wooded ridges. February
sugaring steam, May berry flats, July corn stacked high, crisp low-angle
fall over the sheds. Humid 15-hour summer evenings; gray short winter
days; volatile spring.

## animals (year-round base)

Barn swallows under the market shed eaves; eastern bluebirds on the fence
lines; honeybees working the blossoms; white-tailed deer at the field
edges; wild turkeys in the bottoms; cardinals and juncos trading the
winter; fireflies over the valley floor in July.

## spring (Apr–May)

Place look: valley floor greens first along the river; trillium and
bluebells in the bottom woods; orchard rows white with blossom at Downing;
market sheds open with bedding plants and early greens.
Animals: swallows return to the eaves; ducklings on the Miami; box turtles
out; bees loud in the blossoms.
Looks: chore jackets, rolled sleeves; paper flats of starts; enamel mugs.
Touches: blossom sprig beside the plate; wooden flat staging; farm-name
chalkboard; maple steam still in memory from February.

### maple (Feb carry) — maple brown-sugar oatmeal cups

- Dish: hot maple oatmeal with sugaring-season staging.
- Kodiak product: Protein-Packed Oatmeal (Maple & Brown Sugar).
- Method: stir hot; finish with a still pool from a small pitcher
  alongside (never mid-pour); Dohner maple is the named farm tie.
- Plate dispatch line: "round bowl center frame, still pool across the
  surface, small pitcher alongside, spoon at the right; steam as clean
  white gaps."

### strawberries (May) — Fulton strawberry shortcake waffles

- Dish: May strawberry shortcake over Power Waffles.
- Kodiak product: Power Waffles (Buttermilk).
- Method: halve Fulton Farms May strawberries; stack over one toasted
  waffle with grid lines; spoon the berry pool still over the top.
- Plate dispatch line: "one square waffle with grid lines center plate,
  halved round fruit showing cut faces 5-6 pieces over the waffle, fork
  at the right; each piece one small white highlight gap."

### asparagus + greens — market-green savory cakes

- Dish: savory cakes with shaved market asparagus and Patchwork greens.
- Kodiak product: Buttermilk Power Cakes mix, griddled savory.
- Method: griddle two thick cakes; fan 3-4 long thin asparagus spears in
  parallel over the stack; overlap 2-3 broad flat leaves with center ribs.
- Plate dispatch line: "two thick round cakes center plate, long thin
  spears fanned 3-4 in parallel over the stack, broad flat leaves
  overlapping 2-3 with center rib lines, fork at the right."

## summer (Jun–Aug)

Place look: humid 15-hour evenings; corn walls along the valley roads;
coneflower and black-eyed Susan in the shed beds; market sheds at peak
crowd; haze over the Miami at dawn.
Animals: fireflies thick in July; swallows slicing the shed air; herons on
the river; cicadas; honeybees on the basil blooms.
Looks: short sleeves, straw hats; paper flats; cold drinks in enamel;
checkered cloths.
Touches: corn husks pulled back; whole tomato on the vine beside the
board; dusk-griddle light.

### sweet corn (Jul) — 2nd Street charred-corn cakes

- Dish: charred sweet-corn and scallion savory Power Cakes.
- Kodiak product: Buttermilk Power Cakes mix, griddled savory.
- Method: cut July corn off the cob; fold kernels into batter; griddle
  small thick cakes; fan 3 long thin charred scallions in parallel across.
- Plate dispatch line: "three small thick round cakes center plate,
  small loose kernels scattered 8-10 distinct over the stack, 3 long thin
  spears fanned in parallel across, fork at the right."

### tomatoes (Aug) — market tomato + basil flapjack board

- Dish: tomato wedges and basil over flapjacks for market Saturday.
- Kodiak product: Buttermilk Power Cakes mix.
- Method: stack two cakes; lay tomato wedges showing cut faces 2-3 over
  the stack; overlap broad flat basil leaves 2-3 with center ribs.
- Plate dispatch line: "two round cakes center plate, wedges showing cut
  faces 2-3 over the stack, broad flat leaves overlapping 2-3 with center
  ribs, fork at the right; cut faces white with highlight gaps."

### blueberries — Mile Creek blueberry compote stack

- Dish: blueberry compote over a flapjack stack.
- Kodiak product: Buttermilk Power Cakes mix.
- Method: warm berries until they slump; spoon over a stack of three plain

## fall (Sep–Oct)

Animals: geese skeins over the valley; deer moving at dusk; turkeys in the

Looks: flannel, beanies; wooden crates; burlap; enamelware.
Touches: apple halves showing cut faces; cider press staging; crate
stamps in overlay copy only.

- Kodiak product: Buttermilk Power Cakes mix.

- Plate dispatch line: "stack of three plain round flat cakes center
  plate, 1-inch firm cubes with dense crosshatched edges and white faces
