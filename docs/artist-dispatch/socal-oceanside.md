# artist dispatch — Oceanside, California 92054 (US-CA-OCEANSIDE)

Self-frontier market: San Diego North County coast. Retailers: Target, Vons,
Whole Foods (zip 92054). Audience: North County market families,
Thursday-night crowds — Drew. Message: "Sunset Market frontier — protein
for Pier View Way evenings."

Markets: Oceanside Morning Farmers Market (Thu 9-1, Pier View Way) +
Sunset Market (Thu 5-9, five blocks). Family farms first: Cyclops Farms,
Chino Farm (Rancho Santa Fe), Yasukochi Family Farms (Oceanside),
Community Roots Farm (Oceanside), plus Temecula citrus growers and Valley
Center orchards for citrus/stone fruit. No-retail gap (farm stands, pier
concessions, beach mornings) is served by the Kodiak Cakes subscription
service — never a third-party box.

How to read this file: every recipe ends with a DISPATCH line built on the
plate frame in `docs/plate-dispatch.md` (round plate slightly from above,
short stack of plain round flat cakes center plate, topping arranged over
the stack, fork at right, no hand, no bite, no pour) plus the palette law
(black ink on white only — shapes and counts, never hue). Geometry classes
(cube, wedge, fan, scatter) are the plate-dispatch classes.

Year bird/plant/flower spine: Black-crowned Night Heron / Toyon /
Encelia in winter through Great Egret / Western Sycamore / Coastal
Goldenbush in fall. Buena Vista Lagoon is the flyway anchor all year;
the wooden pier and the Mission San Luis Rey mission-revival skyline are
the built anchors.

## deep winter (Jan-Feb) + Mardi Gras

What the place looks like: low flat winter light, morning marine layer
that burns off by ten. Buena Vista Lagoon full and glassy, pickleweed
mats dark at the edges, winter surf running high past the pier —
white lines stacking to the pilings. Downtown mission-revival arches
(Mission San Luis Rey massing echoed in civic tile and stucco) sit
pale against gray sky. Toyon hedges along the lagoon trail carry full
clusters of hard round berries; coast brittlebush (Encelia) shows first
silver rosettes with early buds.

Animals present: Black-crowned Night Herons stock-still at the lagoon
edge at dusk, American White Pelicans rafting mid-lagoon, coots and
eared grebes in loose flocks, osprey working the channel. Gray whales
southbound offshore. Beach: sanderlings, willets, gulls.

Seasonal looks and touches: wool beanie + market tote mornings; steam
off paper cups; pier planks wet-dark; Christmas-then-bare string lights
down Pier View Way. Mardi Gras touch (Feb): one brass-and-bead night at
the Sunset Market footprint — keep it to table-level beads, never a
costume scene.

### recipe: Satsuma mandarin morning cakes (Jan)

Base: Kodiak Buttermilk Power Cakes, griddled thick. Flesh: Satsuma
mandarin segments from Temecula citrus growers / Valley Center orchards
via the Morning Market — loose-skinned, nearly seedless, January peak.
Method: supreme 2 mandarins into clean wedges; warm 1 tbsp honey with a
squeeze of mandarin juice into a still pool (never mid-pour); fold
chopped fresh mint sparingly. Stack three cakes, shingle 5-7 mandarin
wedges overlapping over the top, finish with the honey pool in a small
pitcher alongside and a mint-leaf pair.

DISPATCH: "stack of three plain round flat cakes center round plate;
5-7 peeled mandarin wedges, curved boat shapes showing open cut faces,
shingled overlapping across the top; small pitcher alongside with still
pool; fork at the right."

## maple / spring (Feb-Mar) + Easter (Apr-May)

What the place looks like: the lagoon wakes up — eelgrass greening,
swallows back over the water, hills behind the mission going briefly
green before summer cures them. Encelia hits full silver-mound bloom
with yellow daisy faces along the coastal bluffs; lupine spikes in the
disturbed edges. Easter morning runs early and cool, marine layer
softening the pier to a silhouette.

Animals present: herons and egrets pairing and stick-carrying at the
lagoon rookery edges; osprey fledging later in the window; gray whales
northbound with calves close offshore (Mar-Apr); brown pelicans
plunge-diving past the surf line; cottontails out at the canyon rims.

Seasonal looks and touches: kite strings over the beach park; Easter
brunch blankets on the grass at the harbor; market stalls switching
from citrus crates to green flats.

### recipe: spring greens + farm-egg savory cakes (Mar-Apr bridge)

Base: Kodiak Buttermilk Power Cakes, griddled thin for a savory build.
Flesh: spring cutting greens + green onions from Cyclops Farms and
Community Roots Farm market stalls; farm eggs. Method: wilt a big
handful of greens in olive oil with sliced green onion; fry eggs sunny;
stack two cakes, lay greens broad and flat, crown with one egg showing
an unbroken white with a liquid-egg still pool (white ring, open
center). Cracked pepper only.

DISPATCH: "two plain round flat cakes center round plate; broad flat
wilted leaves overlapping 2-3 with visible center ribs spread over the
stack; one round fried egg, clean unbroken outline, small open center
gap; fork at the right."

### recipe: Easter citrus-ricotta cakes (Apr-May)

Base: Kodiak Lemon Blueberry or Buttermilk Power Cakes. Flesh: late
Valencia orange + Meyer lemon from Temecula growers; ricotta. Method:
whip ricotta with lemon zest threads; stack three cakes with ricotta
spread between layers; crown with supreme orange wheels and a zest
thread scatter. Easter touch: serve on the harbor grass, blanket
implied by a single folded-cloth rectangle under the plate edge only.

DISPATCH: "stack of three plain round flat cakes center round plate;
round citrus wheels, halves and quarters showing cut faces, 3 pieces
overlapping the top; thin curled zest threads scattered 8-10 distinct;
fork at the right."

## early summer (Jun)

What the place looks like: June gloom — marine layer till noon most
days, the pier dissolving into gray at the seaward end, everything
soft-focus. Then one sudden blue afternoon and the whole beach exhales.
The lagoon sits low and warm; bluff trails edge with chalk dudleya
rosettes going powdery. Bougainvillea starts its long run over
mission-revival walls downtown.

Animals present: California Least Terns (federally listed, real North
County beach nester) working the lagoon mouth and surf line;
brown pelicans in squadron lines; harbor-mouth dolphins most mornings;
leopard-shark pups in the warm shallows off La Jolla pushing north.

Seasonal looks and touches: hoodies tied at waists by noon; surfboard
racks full at dawn patrol; morning-market strawberry flats stacked to
shoulder height.

### recipe: coastal strawberry short-stack (Jun)

Base: Kodiak Strawberry Chocolate Chip or Buttermilk Power Cakes. Flesh:
coastal strawberries, June peak, from Yasukochi Family Farms /
Oceanside morning stalls — small, deep-shouldered, eaten within a day.
Method: hull and quarter 8-10 berries; macerate lightly with a pinch of
sugar 15 minutes; whip cream to soft peaks. Stack three cakes, spoon
berries and their pool over the top, cap with a cream quenelle and one
whole berry with hull.

DISPATCH: "stack of three plain round flat cakes center round plate;
quartered berry wedges showing open cut faces, piled 6-8 over the top;
one smooth cream oval beside the pile; one whole round berry with small
leaf cap at the crown; fork at the right."

## high summer (Jul) + July 4th

What the place looks like: full beach town — hard blue sky once the
layer burns, white midday sand, the pier casting a ruler-straight
shadow, lifeguard towers primary-blue. Mission San Luis Rey's dome and
campanile bleach white at noon. Chalk dudleya at peak powder on the
bluffs; evening brings the first warm Santa Ana teasers off the
Cajon — hot dry breath at sunset.

Animals present: grunion running the night tides (spectators on the
sand, hands-only); brown pelicans dive-bombing bait balls; sea lions
barking off the harbor jetty; ground squirrels bold at every picnic.

Seasonal looks and touches: July 4th fireworks off the pier —
parade morning on Coast Highway, dusk blanket grid on the sand. Corn
signs hand-lettered at Chino Farm's stand.

### recipe: Chino Farm corn + cotija savory cakes (Jul)

Base: Kodiak Buttermilk Power Cakes with 1/2 cup fresh-cut corn folded
in the batter. Flesh: Chino Farm white sweet corn (the famous
unrefrigerated-stand corn — buy it the morning you cook it), July peak.
Method: char 2 ears on the griddle till kernels blister; cut kernels in
slabs; stack three cakes, pile kernels 6-8 tablespoon mounds, crumble
cotija in distinct pea-sized lumps 5-7, lime wedge at plate edge,
cilantro leaves 2-3 flat.

DISPATCH: "stack of three plain round flat cakes center round plate;
small oblong kernels scattered in a distinct handful over the top;
5-7 pea-sized round cheese lumps among the kernels; one wedge with cut
face at the plate edge; 2-3 flat leaves laid on top; fork at the right."

## late summer (Aug)

What the place looks like: warmest water of the year, late golden
light, crowds thinning on weekdays. The hills have cured to straw;
western sycamores along the San Luis Rey riverbed hold green with
mottled peeling trunks. Evenings go pink over the lagoon; first
spiderwebs across the dudleya. Santa Ana season loads on the horizon.

Animals present: egret numbers building at the lagoon; white pelicans
staging; mullet dimpling the channel; night herons reclaiming the
evening shift. Monarchs moving through the coastal corridor.

Seasonal looks and touches: outdoor-movie blankets at the harbor;
tomato boxes heavy at the market; surf wax melting in parked cars.

### recipe: heirloom tomato + basil savory stack (Aug)

Base: Kodiak Buttermilk Power Cakes, griddled thin. Flesh: heirloom
tomatoes, August peak, from Cyclops Farms / morning market tables.
Method: thick-slice 2 tomatoes; salt 10 minutes; stack two cakes,
shingle 3 slices overlapping, lay 2-3 basil leaves flat, drizzle olive
oil as a still pool, flaky salt. No cheese — let the tomato carry it.

DISPATCH: "two plain round flat cakes center round plate; thick round
tomato slices showing open seed-face pattern, 3 pieces shingled
overlapping across the stack; 2-3 broad flat leaves with center ribs
laid on top; fork at the right."

## fall harvest (Sep-Oct) + Halloween

What the place looks like: Santa Ana season — air suddenly crystal,
visibility to San Clemente Island, hot dry afternoons, ash-scented
evenings in bad years (keep smoke OUT of food art; haze only as sky
note). Buena Vista Lagoon turns mirror-calm; southbound migrants
arrive daily. Coastal goldenbush ignites yellow-gold along every bluff
and the lagoon trail; western sycamore leaves go parchment and drop.
Mission-revival stucco glows amber at 5pm. Halloween: carved pumpkins
on Pier View Way porches, corn-maze weekends inland.

Animals present: Great Egrets stalking the shallows in numbers;
white-faced ibis probing the mud; Cooper's hawks working the
songbird flocks; coyotes bolder at the canyon rims at dusk.

Seasonal looks and touches: flannel over tees; market tables stacked
with persimmons like lanterns; cobweb + paper-bat shop windows (keep
props out of the plate frame).

### recipe: Fuyu persimmon + honey cakes (Oct)

Base: Kodiak Honey Oat or Buttermilk Power Cakes. Flesh: Fuyu
persimmons, October peak, from Valley Center orchards via the morning
market — eaten crisp like apples. Method: slice 1-2 Fuyus into thin
half-moons with the star calyx left on one slice; stack three cakes,
fan 4-5 half-moons overlapping across the top, honey still pool in a
small pitcher alongside, toasted pepita scatter 5-7 distinct.

DISPATCH: "stack of three plain round flat cakes center round plate;
thin half-moon slices showing star-pattern center mark, fanned 4-5 in
parallel overlap across the top; 5-7 small flat seed ovals scattered
distinct; small pitcher alongside with still pool; fork at the right."

## Thanksgiving (Nov)

What the place looks like: low golden light, the year's clearest
mornings before the layer rebuilds, lagoon glass green-gold. Night
herons own the dusk again. Sycamore branches go bare and sculptural.
Downtown windows fill with pie signs; the harbor ropes in winter slips.

Animals present: full winter lagoon roster — night herons, great and
snowy egrets, white pelicans, buffleheads and wigeons rafting;
southbound gray whales far offshore late in the month; gull flocks
working the crab boats at the harbor.

Seasonal looks and touches: Friendsgiving blanket spreads; pie steam
on cold windows; crab-boil newspaper tables at the harbor (prop stays
off the plate).

### recipe: Dungeness crab + brown-butter savory cakes (Nov)

Base: Kodiak Buttermilk Power Cakes, griddled small (silver-dollar
size, 5-6 cakes). Flesh: Dungeness crab, November opener, off
Oceanside harbor boats. Method: pick 1 cup lump meat; warm in foaming
butter with lemon threads 90 seconds; arrange the small cakes in a
shingle, pile crab in distinct leg-and-lump pieces 5-7 over the top,
snipped chive lengths 6-8 laid parallel, lemon wedge at plate edge.

DISPATCH: "five small round flat cakes shingled in a row center round
plate; thick white leg-and-lump pieces with hatched undersides, piled
5-7 over the cakes; 6-8 thin straight chive lengths laid parallel on
top; one wedge with cut face at the plate edge; fork at the right."

## winter holidays (Dec) + Christmas

What the place looks like: earliest sunsets, pier lights strung tip
to tail, Oceanside Harbor Parade of Lights reflecting doubled in the
water. Storm surf some weeks — brown water at the river mouth, gulls
screaming. Toyon berries at full red against dark leaves; Encelia
rosettes silvered with rain. Mission San Luis Rey's Christmas Eve
mass lets out into luminaria light.

Animals present: night herons hunched over the lagoon at every dusk;
white pelicans rafted tight; harbor sea lions hauled on the bait
barge; Anna's hummingbirds defending the last bougainvillea.

Seasonal looks and touches: lighted boat hulls; tamale steam at the
Sunset Market holiday edition; pomegranate bowls on every brunch
table.

### recipe: pomegranate + pistachio holiday cakes (Dec)

Base: Kodiak Chocolate Chip or Buttermilk Power Cakes. Flesh:
pomegranates, December peak, North County backyard + market fruit.
Method: seed 1 pomegranate into jewel arils; stack three cakes with
whipped mascarpone between layers; crown with 5-7 spoonfuls of arils
as distinct round beads, shelled pistachio halves 5-7 scattered, one
mint-leaf pair. Holiday touch: serve with the harbor lights behind —
never in the frame.

DISPATCH: "stack of three plain round flat cakes center round plate;
pea-sized round beads scattered 6-8 distinct over the top; 5-7 small
split nut halves among the beads; one pair of small flat leaves with
center ribs at the crown; fork at the right."
