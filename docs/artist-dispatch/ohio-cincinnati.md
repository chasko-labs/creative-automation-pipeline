# artist dispatch — Cincinnati, Ohio 45202 (US-OH-CINCINNATI)

Findlay Market river city. Ohio River valley, humid-continental.
Retailer: Kroger, Target. Audience: river-valley families, Findlay Market
regulars. Message: "Pawpaw capital frontier — protein for Ohio River
mornings."

## sourcing (family farms first)

- Findlay Market, year-round — ramps and morels in spring, strawberries in
  May, sweet corn in July, pawpaws in September, black walnuts in December.
- Ohio River Valley orchards (family farm stands + u-pick where posted).
- Shared frontier calendar: Warren County orchard belt (Lebanon, see
  ohio-lebanon.md) serves Cincinnati — maple February, peaches August,
  apples October, pumpkins November.
- Subscription: the Kodiak Cakes subscription service for rural
  communities — never a third-party CSA.

## place looks (year-round base)

Brick market halls with iron-and-glass sheds, painted Over-the-Rhine
storefronts, the Ohio River bottoms below the hills. Humid summers, crisp
low-angle fall light, gray short winter days, volatile spring.

## animals (year-round base)

Great blue heron and Canada geese on the river; white-tailed deer in the
hillside parks; cardinals and robins; box turtles near the bottoms;
fireflies over the valley floor on summer nights; gray squirrels in the
market trees.

## spring (Apr–May)

Place look: river valley greens up fast; trillium and bluebells carpet the
hillside woods; dogwood and redbud bloom along the streets; market sheds
open their sides to the air.
Animals: robins and cardinals nesting; mallard ducklings on the riverbank;
box turtles out; honeybees on the blossoms.
Looks: light jacket mornings, sleeves up by noon; enamel mugs; canvas
totes full of greens.
Touches: market crate staging; small chalkboard with the farm name; one
sprig of blossom beside the plate, never on the food.

### ramps — wild ramp + cheddar savory Power Cakes

- Dish: savory ramp and sharp cheddar griddle cakes.
- Kodiak product: Buttermilk Power Cakes mix, prepared as thick griddle
  cakes.
- Method: chop ramp greens fine, fold into batter with grated cheddar;
  griddle in a cast-iron skillet until set; serve two stacked with extra
  chopped greens over the top.
- Plate dispatch line: "two thick round cakes center plate, chopped flat
  leaf ribbons scattered 8-10 over the stack, fork at the right; leaves
  drawn white with center rib lines, cake edges dense crosshatching."

### morel mushrooms — morel + butter Power Cakes stack

- Dish: griddled Power Cakes topped with buttered morels.
- Kodiak product: Buttermilk Power Cakes mix.
- Method: halve morels lengthwise, saute in butter until firm with hatched
  undersides; pile over a stack of three plain round flat cakes.
- Plate dispatch line: "stack of three plain round flat cakes center
  plate, thick mushroom slices with gill lines piled 4-5 over the stack,
  fork at the right; firm shapes with hatched undersides, clean unbroken
  outline."

### strawberries (May) — Findlay strawberry shortcake stack

- Dish: strawberry shortcake over Power Cakes.
- Kodiak product: Buttermilk Power Cakes mix.
- Method: halve May strawberries, macerate briefly; stack two cakes, spoon
  berries and their pool over the top; still pool, never mid-pour.
- Plate dispatch line: "two round cakes center plate, halved round fruit
  showing cut faces 5-6 pieces over the stack with one small white
  highlight gap per piece, fork at the right."

## summer (Jun–Aug)

Place look: humid river valley, heavy green canopy; coneflower and
bee balm in the park beds; market sheds deep with corn and tomatoes; haze
over the Ohio at dawn.
Animals: fireflies at dusk; herons fishing the shallows; barn swallows
under the market eaves; cicadas high in the heat.
Looks: short sleeves, straw hats, paper market flats; cold enamel mugs;
checkered cloth under the plate.
Touches: corn husks pulled back as staging; one whole tomato on the vine
beside the board; river-haze soft background.

### sweet corn (Jul) — charred sweet-corn savory cakes

- Dish: sweet-corn and scallion savory Power Cakes.
- Kodiak product: Buttermilk Power Cakes mix, griddled savory.
- Method: cut July corn off the cob, fold kernels into batter with sliced
  scallion; griddle small thick cakes; fan 3 long thin charred scallions
  across the top.
- Plate dispatch line: "three small thick round cakes center plate, small
  loose kernels scattered 8-10 distinct over the stack, 3 long thin spears
  fanned in parallel across, fork at the right; kernels white with one
  highlight gap each."

### blackberries — river-valley blackberry compote cakes

- Dish: blackberry compote over Power Waffles.
- Kodiak product: Power Waffles (Buttermilk).
- Method: warm blackberries until they slump into a spoonable compote;
  spoon over a toasted waffle; keep one small pitcher of the compote pool
  alongside.
- Plate dispatch line: "one square waffle with grid lines center plate,
  pea-sized round berries scattered 5-7 over the waffle, small pitcher
  alongside with still pool, fork at the right."

### tomatoes — market tomato + basil Bear Bites board

- Dish: Bear Bites with market tomato wedges and basil for cubs.
- Kodiak product: Bear Bites (graham).
- Method: cut tomatoes into wedges showing cut faces; arrange Bear Bites
  5-6 alongside with broad flat basil leaves overlapping 2-3.
- Plate dispatch line: "wedges showing cut faces 2-3 pieces one side,
  small bear shapes 5-6 distinct the other, broad flat leaves overlapping
  2-3 with center ribs, no fork — finger food board."

## fall (Sep–Oct)

Place look: aster and goldenrod along the riverbanks and rail cuts; maple
and sycamore color in the hills; Findlay Market stacked with orchard
crates; brick halls warm in low-angle light.
Animals: Canada geese skeins overhead; white-tailed deer moving at dusk;
gray squirrels cutting walnuts; waxwings stripping the last berries.
Looks: flannel, beanies at the door; wooden crates; burlap; enamelware.
Touches: pawpaw halves showing custard faces; walnut shells cracked open;
crate-stamp typography in the overlay layer, never baked into pixels.

### pawpaws (Sep) — pawpaw custard flapjack stack

- Dish: September pawpaw custard over flapjacks (the pawpaw capital
  signature).
- Kodiak product: Buttermilk Power Cakes mix.
- Method: halve ripe pawpaws, scoop the custard flesh; stack three plain
  round flat cakes; spoon custard over the stack; arrange one halved fruit
  showing its cut face at the plate edge.
- Plate dispatch line: "stack of three plain round flat cakes center
  plate, soft spooned custard mounded over the stack, one halved round
  fruit showing cut face at the plate edge, fork at the right; custard
  white with sparse stipple, no fill."

### apples — Ohio apple-cinnamon compote cakes

- Dish: orchard apple-cinnamon compote over Power Cakes.
- Kodiak product: Buttermilk Power Cakes mix.
- Method: dice Ohio River Valley apples thin, simmer with a cinnamon
  whisper; spoon over a stack of three; cinnamon rendered as fine edge
  hatching only.
- Plate dispatch line: "stack of three plain round flat cakes center
  plate, 1-inch firm apple cubes with dense crosshatched edges and white
  faces piled 6-8 over the stack, fork at the right."

### winter squash — roasted squash + pepita Power Waffles

- Dish: roasted squash cubes over Power Waffles with pepitas.
- Kodiak product: Power Waffles (Buttermilk).
- Method: roast squash to 1-inch firm cubes; toast pepitas; pile cubes
  6-8 over a toasted waffle; scatter pepitas 8-10 distinct.
- Plate dispatch line: "one square waffle with grid lines center plate,
  1-inch firm cubes with dense crosshatched edges piled 6-8 over the
  waffle, small loose seeds scattered distinct, fork at the right."

## winter (Nov–Feb)

Place look: gray short days; brick halls lit warm from inside; river fog
on still mornings; evergreen wreaths on the market doors; bare sycamore
and oak silhouettes on the hills.
Animals: cardinals bright against snow; juncos at the feeders; deer
yarding in the parks; geese holding open water.
Looks: wool, heavy mugs, steam; cast iron on the stove; kraft and wood
staging.
Touches: walnut bowls; whole spices as props beside the plate; steam
rendered as clean white gaps, never gray wash.

### black walnuts (Dec) — black-walnut oatmeal bake

- Dish: baked black-walnut and apple oatmeal.
- Kodiak product: Protein-Packed Oatmeal (Maple & Brown Sugar).
- Method: bake the oatmeal thick in a ramekin with diced stored apple and
  chopped black walnuts; serve with walnut halves arranged 3 over the top.
- Plate dispatch line: "round ramekin center frame, halved round nuts
  3 pieces arranged over the top, one small white highlight gap per
  piece, spoon at the right."

### stored apples + squash — spiced snow-morning muffins

- Dish: spiced apple-squash muffins for a gray river morning.
- Kodiak product: Kodiak Muffin mix (Pumpkin Spice or Blueberry base with
  apple folded in).
- Method: fold diced stored apples and squash puree into the batter; bake;
  serve two muffins with one halved showing the crumb.
- Plate dispatch line: "two round muffins center plate, one halved
  showing cut face with stippled crumb, fork at the right; cut faces
  white with open dot texture."

### maple-oat refuge — maple brown-sugar oatmeal cup

- Dish: hot maple oatmeal cup for market mornings.
- Kodiak product: Protein-Packed Oatmeal (Maple & Brown Sugar).
- Method: stir hot; top with a spooned still pool from a small pitcher
  alongside; scatter chopped walnuts 5-6 distinct.
- Plate dispatch line: "round bowl center frame, still pool across the
  surface, small loose nut pieces scattered 5-6 distinct, small pitcher
  alongside, spoon at the right."

## key holidays

### Easter — Easter egg pancake board

- Dish: small pastel-dyed-egg-adjacent pancake minis with strawberries.
- Kodiak product: Buttermilk Power Cakes mix, griddled as minis; Bear
  Bites for cubs.
- Method: griddle silver-dollar minis 5-6; top with halved May
  strawberries showing cut faces; staging carries the Easter cues
  (dogwood sprig, market blossoms), never dyed food.
- Scene: hillside woods with bluebells; morning light; ducklings motif in
  overlay copy only.
- Plate dispatch line: "small round cakes 5-6 overlapping center plate,
  halved round fruit 5-6 pieces over, fork at the right."

### Memorial Day — grill-out peach + granola cakes

- Dish: grilled early-orchard fruit over Power Cakes with granola crunch.
- Kodiak product: Buttermilk Power Cakes mix + Kodiak Granola.
- Method: grill thick fruit slices to firm shapes with hatched undersides;
  lay 2-3 over a stack of two cakes; scatter granola handful distinct.
- Scene: riverbank cookout; herons and swallows; checkered cloth; enamel
  mugs.
- Plate dispatch line: "two round cakes center plate, thick grilled
  slices with hatched undersides 2-3 over the stack, small loose crunch
  scattered distinct, fork at the right."

### July 4th — red-white-and-blueberry stack (shape, never hue)

- Dish: sweet-corn savory cakes plus a berry stack — the cookout double.
- Kodiak product: Buttermilk Power Cakes mix.
- Method: savory corn cakes as in summer entry; alongside, a two-cake
  stack with pea-sized round berries scattered 5-7 and halved strawberries
  showing cut faces. Color is never named — geometry carries it.
- Scene: Ohio River fireworks haze; market corn stacked high; fireflies.
- Plate dispatch line: "two round cakes center plate, pea-sized round
  berries scattered 5-7 plus halved round fruit showing cut faces 2-3,
  fork at the right; all fruit white with highlight gaps."

### Halloween — pumpkin-oat muffin + pawpaw-seed crunch

- Dish: pumpkin-oat muffins with toasted pawpaw-adjacent pepita crunch.
- Kodiak product: Kodiak Pumpkin Spice Muffin mix.
- Method: bake; top each muffin with small loose toasted seeds scattered
  distinct; stage with orchard crates and aster/goldenrod stems beside the
  plate.
- Scene: brick halls, low-angle light, geese overhead; crate staging.
- Plate dispatch line: "two round muffins center plate, small loose seeds
  scattered distinct over each, fork at the right."

### Thanksgiving — black-walnut pumpkin pie bake

- Dish: pumpkin pie bake with black-walnut streusel.
- Kodiak product: Kodiak Pumpkin Spice mix (bake base) + chopped black
  walnuts.
- Method: bake the pie base; crumble walnut streusel of small loose
  pieces over the cut face; serve one wedge showing the cut face.
- Scene: river-valley table; walnut bowls; cardinal at the feeder in the
  background cue.
- Plate dispatch line: "one wedge showing cut face center plate, small
  loose streusel pieces scattered distinct over the face, fork at the
  right; cut face white with stipple."

### Christmas — Christmas morning cinnamon-roll cakes

- Dish: cinnamon-roll Power Cakes with walnut sprinkle.
- Kodiak product: Buttermilk Power Cakes mix with cinnamon swirl.
- Method: griddle three plain round flat cakes with a swirled center line
  set; spoon a still-pool drizzle from a small pitcher alongside (never
  mid-pour); scatter chopped walnuts 5-6.
- Scene: warm brick hall interior; wreath on the door; river fog outside;
  steam as white gaps.
- Plate dispatch line: "stack of three plain round flat cakes with
  center swirl lines, small loose nut pieces 5-6 distinct over the stack,
  small pitcher alongside with still pool, fork at the right."
